# Mycelium Ops: product

**What it is.** One app that joins up a company's sites, people, schedules, security, money and compliance. It sets itself up with a wizard and changes shape to fit the company.

**Who uses it.** First target: large organisations with many sites and hundreds of staff, starting with manufacturers. A design partner (a general operations manager over several factories) reviews the prototype. Everyone in the company uses the same app: staff see their own shifts and tasks; managers see everyone under them; specialists (security, IT, finance, auditors) see their area across the sites they are given.

**The job.** Each person opens it and sees, in one look, what needs them today. One click does the action (approve, assign, fix).

**Why it beats single tools.** One picture of the whole company. Setup is done for you. Every check, data source and AI action is visible and can be changed, and data can stay on the company's own servers.

**Mode.** A working app used every day. Easy scanning, the same controls everywhere and a text version of every picture matter more than decoration.

**Look.** The shared Mycelium look (`DESIGN.md`). This repo holds its reference build.
